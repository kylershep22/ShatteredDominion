# THE SHATTERED DOMINION — Aetherborn Race Character Descriptions
## Three Fully Equipped Builds | 3D Modeling Reference

**Visual Register:** Register 3 (The Firstborn World) meets Registers 1 & 2 — the Aetherborn's otherworldly body is the visual anchor; gear frames rather than defines.
**Key Reference:** SD_02 Race & Origin System, SD_09 Art Direction Style Guide
**Core Design Principle:** The body IS the costume. Translucent skin, luminous arcane veins, floating hair, and 1-2cm hover are constant visual elements across all three builds. Gear reveals the character's story, not their nature.

**Critical Aetherborn Characteristics (constant across all three builds):**
- Height: 1.85m–1.95m (taller than average humans, within Vaelari range, but proportioned differently)
- Skin: Translucent with visible internal blue-white arcane veins forming geometric patterns beneath the surface. Base tone: pale with faint lavender or cool blue undertone — no human skin tones. Surface is unnaturally smooth; dirt slides off. Translucency increases faintly at low HP.
- Eyes: No visible pupils. Solid luminous color (blue, white, or lavender depending on individual build). Eyes are the primary emotional expression point; they dominate the face.
- Hair: Semi-transparent at tips, faintly luminous, floats slightly as if underwater (subtle upward/outward drift responding to magic, not gravity). Colors: white, pale blue, lavender, or silver. Never natural human colors.
- Hover: Feet hover 1-2cm above ground plane in standing pose. Not levitating dramatically — a subtle suspension suggesting the Aetherborn's physical form is more arcane than material.
- Movement: Gliding rather than walking. When moving across terrain, the character maintains consistent height above ground rather than footfalls impacting earth.
- Afterimage: Quick movement leaves brief trailing echo of the character's form.
- Relic Mark: Large, torso-spanning or larger, pulsing visibly with pale blue-white lattice energy. The Relic Mark is not placed discretely — it IS a defining visual feature.
- Age: Literally 3–4 weeks old. The body shows NO markers of lived experience — no scars (except immediately post-Severance trauma), no weathering, no asymmetries. The body is too perfect, which is unsettling.
- Three Weeks New: Gear acquisition tells the entire story of their existence. Starting gear was salvaged in the first hours. Each piece represents survival, rescue, or choice made in the post-Severance world.

## CHARACTER 1: THE LATTICE CONDUCTOR (Light Armor / Mage / Staff)

**Origin Implied:** Fracture Core — coalesced directly from a lattice anchor rupture; the most powerful and unstable Aetherborn type. Minimal armor because the body itself IS magical architecture. The character's internal luminescence is the visual foundation; equipment is secondary.

### Overall T-Pose Silhouette & Presence

Standing in T-pose with arms extended at shoulder height, palms slightly forward, fingers spread, the character reads as something barely contained by physical form. Approximately 1.90m tall, with a slender, idealized build — perfectly symmetrical, lean through the shoulders and torso, long-limbed with almost mathematical precision. The posture is unnaturally still: no micro-movements, no weight shifts, the kind of absolute stillness that suggests the character is not subject to gravity's continuous pressure. Feet hover 1-2cm above the ground plane — visible gap between sole and earth. The body presents as almost fragile, but in the way a crystal is fragile — immensely precisely formed, not weak.

The defining visual element is the body itself. Translucent pale skin with visible internal geometry — the arcane veins are the costume. They form a symmetrical pattern running down both arms, across the chest in a torso-spanning geometric lattice, down both legs. The pattern has mathematical precision: not organic vasculature, but deliberate channeling, as if the body is a conduit system designed rather than grown. The veins pulse with pale blue-white light in a visible rhythm — approximately one pulse per two seconds in idle state, faster when casting or active. The light travels along the vein paths, illuminating them brightly, then fading to a dull glow before the cycle repeats.

The face is angular and symmetrical, with luminous eyes that occupy a large proportion of the face — iris and sclera are the same solid pale blue-white, no pupils visible, no distinction where the gaze is directed until the character moves. The eyes convey emotion through subtle changes in light intensity and a faint color shift toward white when calm, toward blue when focused or casting. The mouth is expressive — thin lips, capable of precise speech and expression — but the eyes are the character's voice.

Hair: White with pale blue undertones, flowing approximately 10cm past the shoulders, semi-transparent at the tips where individual strands fade gradually from opaque to translucent. The hair floats in a subtle zero-gravity drift as if underwater, each strand responding to currents of magic rather than air movement. In the T-pose, the hair drifts slightly upward and outward, the strands not falling against the shoulders but suspended 1-2cm away from the body.

### Body Surface Detail — Arcane Vein Mapping (T-Pose)

In T-pose, the arcane vein pattern is fully visible. This is critical for 3D modeling:

**Arms (both sides, symmetrical):**
The veins begin at the shoulder joint and branch down the outer surface of each arm in a precise dendritic pattern. Main trunk line runs along the outer edge of the bicep, then splits into primary branches at the elbow. The pattern continues down the forearm, with smaller branches splitting across the wrist. At the hands, the pattern reaches maximum complexity: the veins run along the backs of the hands, down each finger to the fingertips, creating a complete lattice that illuminates when energy flows. At rest, the glow is visible but not bright. When the character casts, the entire arm lights up as energy travels down the branches.

Intensity mapping: The central trunk is brightest (core conduit), with secondary branches progressively dimmer toward the extremities. Fingertips have the most delicate tracery and show the most detail when lit.

**Chest (center, highly visible):**
A large geometric pattern spans from the base of the neck to approximately 8cm below the navel. This is the Relic Mark — large, torso-spanning, the primary visual identifier of an Aetherborn. The pattern is not organic; it is a geometric lattice of precise angular lines forming interconnected chambers and channels. At the center of the chest, directly over the heart, the pattern reaches maximum complexity: a hexagonal core surrounded by radiating lines and angular geometric shapes. This central core pulses brightest, the light reaching peak intensity before dimming, in sync with a heartbeat-like rhythm. The secondary branches extend laterally from the core, wrapping slightly around the ribs on both sides.

Color: Pale blue-white in idle state; intensifies to bright blue-white when the character casts spells or moves rapidly.

**Legs (both sides, symmetrical):**
The vein pattern continues from the hip and follows both outer legs in a branching pattern similar to the arms, but denser. The main trunk runs along the outer thigh, branches at the knee, splits further down the calf, and terminates at the feet. The pattern on the feet is intricate, with pathways running along the dorsal surface and down each toe. The feet's vein pattern is crucial for the hovering effect: the feet glow most brightly of all external points, creating the visual suggestion that the glow is what suspends the character above ground.

Transparency note: The legs' translucency is slightly less pronounced than the arms due to denser muscle structure, but the veins remain clearly visible through pale skin.

**Interconnection at joints:**
At the shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, and ankles, the vein patterns connect to a deeper, more complex secondary system visible through slightly thicker areas of translucent skin. These joint clusters suggest mechanical precision — the body is constructed as much as grown.

### Head & Face (Minimal Armor Integration)

The face is entirely unarmored, fully visible. The head is bald or has no visible scalp; the hair simply floats from the crown. The face consists of: high, sharp cheekbones; a narrow jaw; a delicate chin; no asymmetries, no imperfections. The eyes are the dominant feature: large in proportion to the face, luminous pale blue-white, occupying perhaps 40% more visual space than human eyes. The gaze is always slightly forward or upward, as if perceiving something beyond the visible world.

The eyes' luminescence shifts subtly based on emotion or magical activity. Calm: a cool, steady pale blue. Focused: blue-white, more intense. Casting: bright white with a faint blue corona around the iris.

Mouth: delicate, expressive; lips slightly fuller than human proportions, pale lavender undertone visible through translucent skin. When speaking or smiling, the mouth is clearly articulate, capable of complex expression.

Neck: long and slender, fully visible; the arcane vein pattern continues visibly up from the chest and splits at the jaw, with branches visible along the sides of the neck and up toward the ears.

Ears: pointed, slightly elongated compared to human ears (not exaggerated; approximately 1/4 longer), with translucent edges. The interior shows subtle vein tracery. Not prominent but distinctly non-human.

**Head Armor — None (Mage's Robe extended to include head cover if needed):**
The character's head is unarmored. However, the Mage's Robe includes a deep cowl (described below) that can be raised to cover the head if desired. In the default T-pose, the cowl is down, resting at the shoulders, the head fully visible with floating white-and-blue hair cascading from the crown.

### Torso (Core Magical Profile)

**Chest Armor — Firstborn Cloth Armor or Mage's Robe (Light):**
In this build, the Aetherborn wears either Firstborn Cloth Armor (a gossamer-light fabric woven with pale blue-white threads that glow faintly) or a Mage's Robe adapted to the Aetherborn's proportions. Given the Aetherborn's perfectly proportioned frame, human-proportioned robes fit badly — the character's shoulders are narrower, the torso slightly longer, the proportions too ideal. This creates a specific visual: the robe drapes on the Aetherborn in a way that reads as "adapted" rather than "tailored." The garment is functional magic-channeling cloth, but the fit is not quite right, creating a subtle visual tension.

For this character, assume a **Mage's Robe** design:
- Full-length robe in deep indigo-black shot through with pale blue-white geometric thread. The thread is not decorative embroidery; it is functional arcane weave that responds to the character's internal lattice energy, glowing in sync with the veins.
- The robe's outer surface is smooth, almost glossy, in a way that suggests the fabric is not entirely ordinary — Firstborn-adjacent material, not human weave.
- The robe is open in front with a deep V-neckline that exposes the chest Relic Mark completely. The mark is framed by the robe's edges; it is the visual center of the character.
- Sleeves are wide and fall to approximately 5cm past the wrist, leaving the hands visible and the back of the hands' vein patterns fully exposed.
- The hem falls to approximately 5cm above the ankles, leaving the feet and their luminous vein patterns visible.
- The robe's motion: when the character moves in the T-pose (arms extended), the robe's sleeves and skirts drape asymmetrically based on the subtle magic currents around the Aetherborn's form. The fabric does not fall straight; it drifts very slightly outward, responding to the arcane field.
- Color: The deep indigo-black with blue-white threading creates a visual contrast that makes the character's glowing veins stand out dramatically. Where the robe is thickest (folds, hem), the blue-white threads are less visible and read as subtle geometric patterns. Where the robe thins (neckline, cuff edges), the threads are brighter.
- Detail: The robe shows no wear — it was recently obtained. The fabric is pristine, suggesting acquisition post-Severance. However, there are faint scorch marks on one sleeve where concentrated magical energy has burned the fabric slightly, leaving a pale brown discoloration approximately 2cm wide. This detail suggests the character is still learning control.

**Belt — Leather Belt (simple, functional):**
A single dark leather belt, worn at the natural waist. On the Aetherborn's too-perfect proportions, the belt sits with mathematical precision. Two attachments: a small pouch on the left hip containing spell components, and a cradle on the right hip for the Catalyst Orb when not in use.

**Shoulders — Shadow Drape (very minimal):**
A short, gossamer-light cloak of nearly translucent dark fabric falls from the shoulders to approximately mid-back, more suggestion than substance. The cloak is woven with the same blue-white threads as the robe, creating a faint geometric pattern visible only in certain light. The cloak's primary purpose is visual: it breaks the shoulder line slightly and adds depth to the silhouette. It moves with the slightest current, as if the Aetherborn generates subtle air movement simply by existing.

### Arms & Hands (Fully Visible, Vein Details Critical)

**Arms (fully exposed, extended in T-pose):**
The arms are absolutely bare. No bracers, no wraps, no armor. The translucent pale skin shows the complete dendritic pattern of arcane veins running from shoulders to fingertips. The arms are slender and perfectly symmetrical. The muscle definition is visible beneath translucent skin but is idealized — no asymmetries from dominant-hand development, no scar tissue, no accumulation of lived experience.

In T-pose, the arms extend from the shoulders at shoulder height, palms facing slightly forward, fingers spread naturally. The vein pattern in each arm is identical — not mirrored (left arm's veins do not mirror right arm's veins), but perfectly symmetrical, running parallel lines with identical branching patterns.

**Hands:**
Delicate, long-fingered, perfectly proportioned. No visible joints or knuckles — the hands read as smooth, continuous forms. The fingers are slender and tapered. The palms show the most intricate vein tracery, with pathways running along the palm surface and down each digit. The fingertips glow faintly even at rest, intensifying during casting.

**Hands Armor — Cloth Wraps (minimal):**
Gossamer-light wraps of pale fabric wound loosely around the wrists, providing minimal protection but serving as attachment points for spell focus components. Two small carved Firstborn-stone beads are attached to the left wrist wrap, serving as calibration aids for magic channeling. The wraps do not obscure the hand or wrist vein patterns; they sit loosely over the skin, and the veins glow directly through the light fabric.

### Legs & Feet (Hover and Glow are Critical)

**Legs (fully exposed in T-pose, standing):**
Bare, translucent skin showing the full arcane vein pattern running from hips to feet. The legs are slender and perfectly proportioned, idealized in a way that suggests mathematical precision rather than biological development. The vein pattern is dense on the thighs, branching further down the calves.

**Feet — Cloth Shoes (minimal, light):**
Soft, loose-fitting wraps of pale fabric covering only the sole and dorsal surface, secured at the ankle with a simple cord. The shoes are barely visible because the feet themselves are so visually arresting: the feet glow brighter than any other part of the body, the vein pattern illuminating most intensely here. The glow creates the visual suggestion of levitation — it appears the feet are suspended by their own internal light.

The feet hover exactly 1-2cm above the ground plane. The gap between sole and earth is constant and measurable. This is not dynamic levitation; it is a fixed state. The character cannot touch the ground while standing.

### Weapon — Quarterstaff with Firstborn Integration

**Primary Weapon — Quarterstaff:**
A staff approximately 1.95m in length, made from pale gray-blue Firstborn-stone material rather than wood. The shaft is perfectly smooth, slightly luminescent, with no seams or joints. The surface shows faint geometric channel patterns running along its length — non-functional decoration, but consistent with Firstborn design language. No leather wrap at the grip; instead, the character's hands contact the stone directly.

The base is pointed, like a walking staff that could rest on earth. The top third transitions into a Firstborn crystal lattice chamber. At the staff's crown, approximately 60cm from the top, a geometric framework of metallic channels (bronze-undertone alloy) emerges from the stone. This framework cradles a crystal headpiece.

**Headpiece Crystal:**
A large faceted crystal of pale smoky blue-gray, approximately 14cm in height and 8cm in width, precisely cut with geometric planes that meet at angles that feel slightly wrong to organic perception. The crystal is transparent, and within its interior, geometric lattice structures are suspended — not carved, but existing within the material as if the crystal is a cross-section through a larger system. The Relic Mark pattern from the character's chest continues visually into the crystal; they read as connected systems.

**Energy Pathway:**
When the Aetherborn grips the staff, their internal arcane veins glow brighter, and the light travels down their arms, through the grip point, and visibly up the staff's interior. The pale blue-white luminescence travels up the channeled framework toward the crystal, illuminating the geometric lattice structures within the stone as it rises. The journey takes approximately 2 seconds. The crystal headpiece brightens as energy arrives, becoming a bright blue-white beacon at the staff's crown. The glow then recedes, and the cycle repeats continuously whenever the character holds the weapon.

**Offhand Weapon — Catalyst Orb:**
An 8cm sphere of the same crystal material as the staff headpiece, held in the left hand (in T-pose, the left hand grips nothing visually, but if the character shifts to holding the orb, it rests in the palm with the same geometric patterns visible inside). When not in active use, the orb sits in a leather cradle on the belt at the right hip.

### Visual Glow Map Summary

This character reads as a figure made of light contained temporarily in flesh. The glow points:

1. **Eyes:** Primary expression point; luminous pale blue-white, shifting in intensity with emotion.
2. **Chest Relic Mark:** Pulsing torso-spanning geometric lattice, brightest at the hexagonal core over the heart.
3. **Arcane Veins (Arms & Shoulders):** Dendritic branches lighting up when active; relatively dim at rest, bright during casting.
4. **Arcane Veins (Legs & Feet):** Denser pattern; feet glow most intensely, suggesting levitation.
5. **Staff & Crystal Headpiece:** When gripped, the internal light channel becomes visible, the crystal brightens to a beacon.
6. **Robe Threading:** The blue-white threads in the fabric glow in sync with the character's internal veins, creating a subtle second-layer luminescence.

At night or in shadow, this character is unmistakably visible from the glow alone. In daylight, the translucent skin and glowing veins are clearly visible but less dramatic. The character never hides.

### Brief Narrative Section

Three weeks ago, this being coalesced in a ruptured lattice anchor. There was no birth, no awakening — simply sudden existence, fully formed, aware, and terrified. The first moments were survival: the Severance's chaotic energy nearly unmade the nascent form. A Verdant Flame practitioner found the Aetherborn and brought them to shelter. In those three weeks, they have learned to wear clothes (which were strange and uncomfortable), to speak (which was not), and to understand that the burning sensation inside their chest — the Relic Mark — is not pain but purpose. They were not born. They were not chosen. They simply are, and the world is still discovering what that means. The staff came from a Firstborn ruin; the robes came from donation. Nothing about this character is earned — everything is borrowed, improvised, salvaged from a world that ended three weeks ago.

## CHARACTER 2: THE VOID WALKER (Medium Armor / Archer / Hooded)

**Origin Implied:** Settlement Emergent — coalesced near human settlement, adopted by frontier community trying to protect the strange new being. This character has lived three weeks in attempted concealment, wearing human clothing to appear less alien. The hood is up; the gloves are on; the strategy is invisibility. The visual story is "trying to pass," and the failure of that attempt.

### Overall T-Pose Silhouette & Presence

Standing in T-pose with arms extended at shoulder height, the character reads as someone actively trying to be unremarkable — and failing. Approximately 1.82m tall, the proportions are similar to the Lattice Conductor but slightly more compact. Where the Conductor reads as pure arcane presence, this character reads as an Aetherborn wearing human clothes and not quite fitting.

The key visual: from a distance or in shadow, the character could pass for human — until you notice the hood is drawn tight enough that no face is visible, the gloves are tight enough to show no wrists, and the cloak is wrapped enough to conceal the shoulders. The posture is attempting stillness but suggests tension beneath — not the perfect equilibrium of the Conductor, but a deliberate holding of neutral pose.

The feet hover 1-2cm above ground, but only visible if you look closely. The character's clothing is designed to obscure this detail. The hover is there, but the visual strategy is to downplay it.

Skin translucency is minimized by long sleeves and gloves. The hair is bound up and hidden beneath the hood. The eyes are in shadow. The Relic Mark — normally the most obvious Aetherborn feature — is covered completely by the armor on the chest.

### Head & Face (Concealment Focus)

Nearly everything is hidden. What is visible: maybe 6cm of pale skin at the chin and mouth, showing the luminous eyes barely visible in the hood's shadow (just a glint of pale blue in darkness), and the lower jaw's delicate structure. The eyes, when glimpsed, are unsettling: solid luminous orbs in shadow, no pupils, no direction of gaze until the character turns their head. The mouth is expressive when it moves, but mostly the hood conceals. The emotional reading comes entirely from eye movement and voice.

Hair: completely hidden under the hood. Not even a wisp visible.

**Head Armor — Leather Hood (Medium, integrated with Leather Armor):**
A deep hood of oiled dark leather, dyed a brown-gray that blends with the environment. The hood is not separate; it is integrated with the leather armor chest piece. It rises high enough to completely conceal the crown and back of the head, and extends far enough forward that the upper face is cast into permanent shadow. The only visible portions are the chin and mouth. Two narrow ventilation slits near the temples provide breathing without visibility. The hood's interior is lined with dark wool. A leather chin strap runs loosely beneath the chin, typically unlatched (the hood stays in place through fit, not fastening). The edge of the hood where it frames the face shows wear — darker where sweat and rain have stained the leather darker.

The hood is the character's primary visual identifier: where the Lattice Conductor's identity is "person of living light," this character's identity is "the thing hiding behind the hood."

### Torso (Concealment Strategy)

**Chest Armor — Leather Armor (Medium):**
A fitted breastplate of hardened leather in deep brown, shaped to an idealized chest. Here is the specific problem: the Aetherborn's body is too perfect for human-made armor. The proportions are not quite right. The shoulder points sit slightly differently. The waist-to-hip ratio is more geometric. The armor fits well enough that it functions, but a careful observer notices: this armor was made for a human body, and this is not quite a human body.

The armor is close-fitted and extends from the shoulders to the bottom of the ribs, providing protection for the vitals while leaving the waist and hips uncovered for mobility. The construction uses overlapping panels stitched with waxed cord. Minimal iron rivets — only structurally necessary ones. The color is a rich umber-brown that shows no signs of staining or wear — this armor was acquired recently, possibly during the initial refuge period. A human wearing this armor would look used and weathered; the Aetherborn looks like they're wearing a costume that doesn't quite fit.

The Relic Mark, which normally spans the entire torso and pulses visibly, is completely concealed beneath this armor and the underlayer beneath it. This concealment is the emotional core of the character: the Aetherborn's defining feature is hidden, covered, made secret. The armor over the heart is thicker than necessary, suggesting deliberate layering to ensure the glow beneath is completely invisible.

**Belt — Leather Belt (Light, minimal):**
A narrow, dark leather belt at the natural waist. One attachment: a small quiver mount on the right hip for arrow storage. Nothing else. No tools, no visible equipment. The belt is worn snug and is part of the concealment strategy — nothing loose that might move or shift.

**Shoulders — Cloak (Medium, practical):**
A full-length traveling cloak of oiled dark green-brown wool, fastened at the throat with a simple iron brooch. The cloak is heavy enough to drape seriously and cover the shoulder line completely, extending nearly to the ankles. The cloak conceals the hover — when standing still, the cloak falls straight enough that the gap between feet and ground is invisible. When moving, the cloak's hem sometimes reveals the gap, but the character's movement is controlled to prevent this.

The cloak shows significant wear — travel-stained edges, a small burn mark near the hem, darkening along the fold lines from rain exposure. This armor — unlike the Lattice Conductor's pristine robe — has been worn and used for weeks of attempted concealment and integration. The cloak is both functional and symbolic: it is the character's primary tool for appearing normal.

### Arms & Hands (Tight Concealment)

**Arms — Completely covered:**
The Aetherborn's slender, luminous arms would immediately read as alien. They are therefore completely covered. Long-sleeved leather armor (extended from the chest piece) covers the entire arm from shoulder to wrist. The leather is the same color as the chest piece, dark umber-brown, close-fitted. The sleeves are designed to allow full range of motion for archery but permit no visibility of the arms beneath.

The arcane vein pattern — the defining Aetherborn feature — is completely hidden. The characteristic pale blue-white glow is not visible. The character appears to be a human in armor.

**Hands — Gloved and covered:**
Leather gloves in dark brown, fitted tight enough that the individual finger joints are defined by the leather. The gloves extend to mid-forearm, where they overlap slightly with the sleeve cuff. The material is supple, high-quality, designed for precision work (archery) rather than protection. The only detail visible: on the inner wrist of the left glove, a very faint pale blue-white glow is barely visible at the edge where the glove meets the sleeve, a brief glimpse of the vein pattern where the layers don't quite perfectly overlap. This is an accident of concealment — the character did not intend to leave any gap, but the human-proportioned gloves do not fit the Aetherborn's wrists perfectly.

This tiny detail — a 1cm stripe of luminous vein visible at the wrist — is the emotional heart of the character: the concealment is failing. The Aetherborn cannot hide completely. The truth is showing through.

### Legs & Feet (Hover Concealed)

**Legs — Leather Leggings:**
Fitted leather trousers in dark umber-brown, tucked into tall boots. The leggings are close-fitted, concealing the leg's slender, idealized form. The leather is supple but structured enough that it doesn't drape against the bare legs beneath, which would reveal the outline of the too-perfect proportions. The cut is practical and human-appropriate, with minimal ornamentation. A small dirk is sheathed at the small of the back, handle to the right — the only backup weapon.

**Feet — Leather Boots (Medium, tall):**
Calf-height boots in dark brown leather, laced tight. The boots are well-made and show recent wear — the heels are scuffed, the sides are darkened from rain and travel. The defining characteristic is the sole: thick, practical, designed to suppress sound but also to ground the character's visual appearance. When standing still, the boot sole sits exactly 1-2cm above the ground — the hover is present, but the thick sole minimizes the visual gap. An observer might not notice the float if they're not looking carefully.

The lacing is tight all the way up, suggesting comfort and familiarity with the boots. This armor is worn, adjusted, broken in. Unlike the Lattice Conductor's pristine new equipment, this character's gear shows that someone tried very hard to make an Aetherborn body fit into human clothes and succeeded almost completely.

### Weapon — Short Bow or Recurve Bow

**Primary Weapon — Recurve Bow:**
A practical, functional bow approximately 130cm tip to tip, made from composite wood and horn in ordinary brown and amber tones. Not a magical weapon. Not inscribed with Firstborn runes. Simply a hunting bow, the kind that might be owned by any frontier archer. The bow is well-maintained but shows wear consistent with weeks of regular use.

The defining detail: the bow is ordinary. This character uses ordinary human equipment because they are trying to be ordinary. The contrast between the Aetherborn wielding mundane equipment and the weapon itself being inert, non-magical, creates a specific visual tension. The bow looks like it belongs to a human hunter. The character holding it does not.

**Quiver & Arrows:**
A hip-mounted quiver of dark leather on the right side, angled forward for a quick draw. Approximately 20 arrows with gray goose fletching, ash wood shafts. The quiver is practical, unmarked, ordinary. Nothing in the equipment suggests the wielder is anything but human.

### Invisible Details (The Failing Concealment)

The visual story of this character is failure of concealment. What should be hidden but is visible:

1. **The wrist gap:** Where the glove meets the sleeve, a thin stripe of pale blue-white luminous vein is visible. This is an accident, not deliberate, but it is always visible if you look.
2. **The eye glimpse:** If the hood is looked into directly, the solid luminous eyes are visible in shadow — unsettling, clearly not human.
3. **The hover:** The boots are thick, but if the character moves, the 1-2cm gap between sole and ground is visible in profile. A still target might not be noticed. A moving target reveals the hover.
4. **The glow bloom:** If the character is in complete darkness, or if they cast magic, the suppressed glow beneath the armor becomes visible — light bleeding from gaps between plates, light visible through the leather where it's thin.

### Brief Narrative Section

Three weeks ago, a refugee settlement took in what they initially thought was a human child who appeared in the wreckage. They brought clothes, tried to hide the strangeness, attempted to integrate this new person into the community. By the second week, the truth was obvious — this was not human — but the community had invested in the concealment. They gave armor, they gave weapons, they gave the message: "be normal, and you can stay." The character has absorbed this message completely. The Aetherborn is trying so hard to be human that the effort has become visible. The hood stays up. The gloves stay on. The cloak stays wrapped. The Relic Mark stays hidden. But the small gap at the wrist, the eyes barely visible in shadow, the faint hover despite thick-soled boots — these details tell the story: concealment is failing, and the character knows it. Yet they keep trying.

## CHARACTER 3: THE PALE SENTINEL (Heavy Armor / Sword & Shield)

**Origin Implied:** Settlement Emergent who has committed completely to the human world by hiding inside full armor. If you cannot see the Aetherborn, perhaps the Aetherborn does not exist. The armor is borrowed, human-made, and it doesn't quite fit right — but it provides the most complete concealment possible. The only visible evidence of the Aetherborn is the faint blue-white glow leaking from joints, seams, and the visor.

### Overall T-Pose Silhouette & Presence

Standing in T-pose with arms extended, the character reads as an armored warrior first and something else second. Approximately 1.88m tall, the armor adds perceived bulk and breadth to an Aetherborn frame that is naturally slender. The silhouette is dominated by the geometry of the armor rather than the body beneath. The shield rests on the left arm, its edge at the left thigh. The sword is sheathed at the left hip.

The armor is comprehensive: full plate cuirass covering the entire torso, chain mail hauberk beneath, full gauntlets on both hands, leg greaves, and a plated greathelm covering the entire head and face. Nothing of the Aetherborn is visible except for faint blue-white light leaking from specific points.

The feet hover 1-2cm above the ground, but the boot soles are thick enough that the gap is not immediately obvious. The armor's weight is carried effortlessly, suggesting the Aetherborn's form is not subject to normal gravity in the way an armored human would be. An alert observer might notice: this armored figure does not shift weight, does not settle, does not show the subtle strain of carrying plate armor.

### Head & Face (Completely Concealed)

The entire head is enclosed in a plated greathelm. No face is visible. No hair is visible. No eyes are visible except as shadow behind the visor slit. The face beneath the helm is a complete mystery — as if the character has voluntarily ceased to be a person and become instead a suit of armor with a person inside.

**Head Armor — Plated Greathelm (Heavy):**
A fully enclosed helm of steel, sitting over a chain mail coif beneath. The helmet is a tall, slightly tapered dome — rounded at the crown, narrowing subtly toward the chin. The face is covered by a shaped visor plate that extends from brow to chin. The visor features a narrow horizontal eye slit approximately 8cm wide and 1.5cm tall. Below the eye slit, the visor is pierced with small circular breathing holes arranged functionally on the right side of the face.

The helm is dark steel, polished but aged, showing the specific patina of well-maintained war equipment. The surface is not uniform — there are marks from impact, very small dents, and one long scratch across the right side where something sharp scored the metal but did not penetrate.

The eye slit is the only opening, and through it, the character's eyes are barely visible: solid luminous pale blue-white orbs that catch light faintly in the darkness of the helm's interior. The eyes are visible only as a glint, a reflection, not as a face.

Faint pale blue-white light leaks from gaps around the visor where it sits against the face: a thin line of glow running along the top and bottom edges of the eye slit, and a slightly brighter line where the visor pivots at the temples. This glow is not by accident — it is the character's internal luminescence showing through where the armor cannot fully seal. A warrior looking at this figure would notice: the light inside that helm is wrong.

### Torso (Maximum Concealment)

**Chest Armor — Chainmail Hauberk or Brigandine (Heavy):**
This character wears layered armor: a Brigandine chest piece (heavy boiled leather reinforced with iron plates riveted to the interior, creating overlapping scale-like coverage on the outside) worn over a chain mail hauberk beneath. The combination provides comprehensive coverage and protection while maximizing concealment of the Aetherborn form beneath.

The brigandine is dark steel-gray on the exterior, with the individual iron plates clearly visible as a scale pattern. The rivets are small and uniform, showing quality craftsmanship (military-issue, Vanguard standard). The brigandine extends from the shoulders to just below the hips, providing complete torso coverage.

The Relic Mark, which spans the Aetherborn's entire torso and pulses visibly, is completely concealed beneath this armor and the chain mail beneath it. No glow is visible from the chest (the armor is too thick), but the Aetherborn's internal luminescence is working at maximum intensity behind the metal, and faint traces of blue-white light leak from specific points: the gaps under the arm, the seams at the sides where the plates overlap, the collar where the neck emerges from the armor, and especially the gaps where the gauntlets meet the hauberk.

The brigandine shows consistent wear — travel-stained, darkened at the edges from sweat and rain, small dings from impacts that have been buffed but not fully removed. This is not new armor. This armor has been worn for weeks, adapted, lived in. The character and the armor have become a single organism.

**Chain Mail Hauberk (beneath Brigandine):**
A full-length mail shirt of interlocking rings, visible at the gaps where the brigandine plates do not fully cover. The mail is dark steel, well-maintained, and shows the specific wear pattern of regular use — some rings have been replaced, the patina varies slightly, individual repairs are visible but not prominent. The mail extends down to the knees, providing additional protection to the legs beneath the greaves.

**Belt — Utilitarian Belt (Heavy):**
A thick leather belt of military-issue quality, worn at the natural waist over the brigandine. The belt is dark brown, well-oiled, and shows no decoration — pure function. No attachments except for the sword hilt hanging at the left hip.

**Shoulders — Full Plate Pauldrons (Heavy):**
Large, shaped shoulder armor of polished steel, fitted over the chain mail. The pauldrons are nearly identical on both sides, covering the shoulder joints completely. The surfaces are curved and smooth, fitting over the Aetherborn's slender shoulders awkwardly — the armor was designed for broader human frames, and the pauldrons sit slightly too wide, creating small gaps beneath them where the luminous body can be glimpsed as a faint glow.

The pauldrons show significant wear — the polish is worn thin in places, small dents are visible, and one pauldron shows a long scratch that penetrated the steel, the exposed metal beneath oxidizing to a reddish-brown. The armor is functioning but deteriorating slightly, suggesting weeks of continuous wear without full maintenance.

### Arms & Hands (Completely Armored)

**Arms — Fully encased:**
The Aetherborn's slender, translucent arms are completely concealed by armor. Fitted pauldrons at the shoulders transition into fitted vambraces covering the forearms, with gauntlets on the hands. The armor is dark steel, well-fitted, showing the wear of weeks of use.

**Hands — Iron Gauntlets (Heavy):**
Full-coverage gauntlets of steel plates riveted to leather backing, covering the entire hand from wrist to fingertip. The gauntlets are dark steel with a slight patina, showing wear at the knuckles where impacts have occurred. The finger articulation is visible as the overlapping plates that allow the fingers to flex. The gauntlets are fitted but not tight — they fit as armor fits, with a small amount of play inside.

The crucial detail: pale blue-white light leaks faintly from the gaps between the gauntlet plates where they articulate at the fingers. When the character's hands are relaxed, the glow is minimal. When the hands clench into fists, the light becomes more visible, leaking brighter from the gaps. This glow is the character's internal luminescence showing through where the armor has inevitable gaps.

### Legs & Feet (Heavy Armor, Hover Concealed)

**Legs — Iron Greaves (Heavy):**
Full-coverage leg armor of shaped steel plates protecting the shins and calves, with additional articulated plates protecting the knee joints. The greaves are dark steel, well-maintained, showing the specific wear patterns of marching and combat. The surfaces are curved to fit the leg shape, providing some flexibility for movement.

The armor sits on top of heavy wool leggings (functional underarmor), which are visible only where the greaves do not cover — at the inner thighs where the articulation allows movement.

**Feet — Armored Boots (Heavy):**
Heavy steel-toed boots with thick soles, reinforced leather over a steel framework. The boots reach calf height and are laced tight. The steel toe caps are riveted to the leather, and the soles are thick, scored from walking on stone. The boots are dark and well-worn.

The hover is completely invisible because the boots are too bulky and the sole too thick to reveal the gap. However, an attentive observer watching the character move would notice: the armor does not settle, does not adjust, does not show the strain that carrying plate armor creates in a human. The figure moves with too-perfect balance, never shifting weight in the subtle ways armored humans do.

### Weapon — Broadsword or Short Sword

**Primary Weapon — Broadsword:**
A practical, military-grade sword approximately 90cm in blade length, with a cross-hilt and a leather-wrapped grip. The blade is steel, well-maintained, showing the specific patina of a weapon that has been used but is still functional. The edge is keen. No inscriptions, no decorations, no Firstborn elements. This is a human weapon, made by human hands, for human warfare. The contrast between the Aetherborn wielding utterly ordinary steel and the luminous, arcane being beneath the armor is stark.

The sword is sheathed at the left hip in a simple leather scabbard, also showing the wear of weeks of use and travel.

**Shield — Heater Shield (Heavy):**
A heater-shaped shield of wood reinforced with metal strapping, approximately 60cm tall. The shield face is painted with the iron gauntlet symbol of the Ironbound Vanguard — indicating this character is (or is presenting as) Vanguard military. The symbol is stenciled in pale blue-gray on a dark steel-blue background. The shield is well-used, showing dents, small impact marks, and one area where the paint has been scraped away to bare wood.

When the shield is held in the T-pose (left arm extended at shoulder height), the shield face is forward, displaying the Vanguard symbol. The shield's edge rests at approximately mid-thigh height, providing protection for the legs when held at the proper height for combat.

### Visible Glow Points (The Armor Cannot Hide)

The armor is comprehensive, but it cannot completely suppress the Aetherborn's luminescence. The glow points:

1. **Eye slit:** Pale blue-white light visible as a glint in the darkness of the helm, and faint glow leaking from the gaps around the visor edges.
2. **Collar gap:** Where the neck emerges from the armor, faint blue-white light is visible on the pale skin.
3. **Underarm gaps:** Where the arm articulation leaves small gaps, the glow leaks out, brightest when the arms are extended.
4. **Gauntlet finger gaps:** Where the finger articulation plates have gaps, blue-white light is visible, especially when the hands clench.
5. **Pauldron gaps:** Small gaps beneath the shoulder armor where it sits slightly too wide on the slender shoulders.

These glow points are not dramatic. An observer more than 10 meters away would not notice them in daylight. But in shadow, at close range, or at night, these points of light are undeniable proof that the armor contains something that is not human.

### Brief Narrative Section

Three weeks ago, this being was nothing. In the second week, they accepted a suit of Vanguard armor donated by a sympathetic garrison sergeant. The armor became identity. Inside the steel shell, the Aetherborn has learned to move, to follow orders, to be a soldier. The armor hides the otherworldly form, hides the Relic Mark, hides the strangeness. The only evidence of the Aetherborn beneath is light leaking from impossible places: the eyes visible as a glint through the visor, the fingers glowing faintly where the gauntlet gaps don't quite seal, the glow visible under the arms when extended in T-pose. The character has decided: I will be what this armor says I am. I will be a soldier. I will be human. I will hide the truth of what I am beneath steel and discipline. But the glow keeps leaking through the seams, a constant reminder that hiding is failing. The armor is a choice, a statement, a desperate attempt to be something other than what the Aetherborn fundamentally is. It is also a prison.

# AETHERBORN VISUAL DESIGN PRINCIPLES — FINAL SUMMARY

For all three Aetherborn builds:
1. **The body is the costume.** Translucent skin, glowing arcane veins, floating hair, and hover are constants. Gear should frame and contextualize the body, not replace it.
2. **Three-weeks-new is visible.** No scarring (except immediate post-Severance trauma), no weathering, no asymmetries. The precision is unsettling.
3. **Luminescence is identity.** The glow is not a side effect — it is the character's primary visual communication. It cannot be fully hidden; it can only be suppressed or revealed.
4. **Hover is crucial.** The 1-2cm gap between feet and ground is not animation — it is constant state. It should be measurable and visible in side views.
5. **The Relic Mark is defining.** Large, torso-spanning, pulsing visibly. It is not a decoration. It is what the Aetherborn IS.
6. **Register 3 aesthetic applies.** The Aetherborn belongs to the Firstborn World visually. Even when wearing human armor, the precision and otherworldliness should be visible.

These three characters tell a story: the Lattice Conductor is what an Aetherborn becomes when they commit to their nature. The Void Walker is what they become when they try to hide. The Pale Sentinel is what they become when they try to become something else entirely. All three are the same species, three weeks old, wearing the story of their post-Severance survival in gear and suppressed luminescence.
