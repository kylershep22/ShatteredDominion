## CHARACTER 1: VERDANT FLAME MAGE (LIGHT ARMOR / STAFF)

### Overview
- **Race:** Vaelari (Verdant Flame origin implied)
- **Height:** 2.1m (standing barefoot)
- **Build:** Ectomorphic. Lean shoulders. Long, graceful limbs slightly elongated past human proportions. Fingers tapered and slender. Neck subtly elongated — approximately 8% longer than human baseline.
- **Pose Reference:** T-stance. Arms extended horizontally at shoulder height, palms facing downward, fingers spread naturally. Legs shoulder-width apart, feet planted firmly. Head facing forward.
- **Silhouette:** Vertical emphasis. Robes flow downward; shoulders narrow. Staff extends the vertical line upward. Overall impression: deliberate, ethereal, grounded.

### Head & Face
**Facial Structure:**
- Face template: Angular. High, pronounced cheekbones. Narrow jaw tapering to a subtle point at chin.
- Skin tone: Pale ivory with cool undertones. Faintly luminous quality in rendered low-light (subsurface scattering applied across face, neck, visible forearms).
- Vein patterns: Subtle marble-like striations visible on temples and along neck — approximately 15% darker than surrounding skin (pale blue-gray, #E8E6F0 → #D4D0E8 vein color).
- Eyes: Silver-green with faint luminescence (pupil color #7FB699, iris luminous halo #A8D5BA). Pupils vertically elongated (1.3x human pupil height). Glowing quality suggests active Communion connection.
- Ears: Tapered, pointed. Approximately 6.5cm length (2.5cm longer than human ear). Fine cartilage detail. Pale with faint internal luminescence visible through thin areas.
- Hair: Ash-blonde, fine texture, straight with gentle wave. Metallic sheen visible in catch light (silver-white highlights, base tone #D4B89F). Post-Severance aging: approximately 15% strand dulling, scattered silver strands (mainly at temples and left side of part). Length: waist-length when unbound; currently worn in single long braid with woven vine fiber accent (approx. 4mm diameter natural fiber cord, tan color #C2A878).
- Expression: Still. Serene. Eyes hold depth.

**Head Armor — Verdant Circlet (Light):**
- Construction: Organic bronze-copper circlet, ~4mm diameter, wrapped in pale-green plant fiber (treated nettle cord, slightly iridescent). Winding pattern: spiraling helix starting at left temple, wrapping across crown, ending at right temple.
- Attachment: Two small bronze clasps with leather backing (chamois-tan, #D4A574) at both temples. Secured around head with adjustable fiber tie at nape.
- Detail: Central point on brow features small carved pale jade stone (3mm diameter, color #B5D9C1) hanging as a single accent.
- Fit: Sits lightly; does not cover hair. Faint impression on brow visible where stone sits.

### Torso
**Chest Armor — Arcanist's Robe (Light):**
- Base construction: Three-layer natural linen tunic (undyed, natural fiber, off-white #F5F3EF). Single garment, floor-length.
  - Layer 1 (inner): Fine linen, cream tone (#F5F3EF), undecorated, provides base silhouette.
  - Layer 2 (mid): Heavier linen, slight green tint from natural dye (#E8F1E6), overlaid across shoulders and torso, sleeves extend to wrist.
  - Layer 3 (outer): Sheer silk-equivalent over-layer (pale sage, #D4E8D9) with hand-embroidered geometric lattice pattern in muted blue thread (#A8C9D9). Pattern: interlocking diamond motifs across chest and upper back, suggesting Firstborn design language (angular, precise, geometric rather than organic).

- Sleeve construction: Long, fitted from shoulder to elbow, then flaring gently to wrist. Internal cuff detail: pale blue linen undercuff (2cm visible), visible when hand extends or rotates. Hand slits at wrist (7cm opening) allow movement in gloves.

- Underbelt worn over robe: Hemp cord, natural tan (#C2A878), 2cm wide, knotted at left hip with fabric tail hanging to mid-thigh.

**Shoulders — Grove Mantle (Light):**
- Construction: Full mantle worn over both shoulders. Heavy wool (undyed, natural charcoal-gray #4F5159), draped loose. Length: extends from shoulder to mid-calf on standing figure.
- Attachment: Single clasp at center chest — carved wooden toggle (pale aspen wood, #E8E6E0) with leather backing. Mantle fastens via braided wool cord (natural gray-brown) through toggle.
- Drape detail: In T-pose, mantle hangs vertically with natural folds at sides. Approximately 8 major fold lines on each side where weight settles. Hood integrated but typically worn down; visible as raised fabric at nape.
- Edge detail: Unfinished raw-edge hem. Slight fraying visible. Practical, not decorated.
- Color variation: Natural wool shows variation in fiber tone (range #4F5159 to #5A5F67), giving lived-in, hand-dyed appearance.

### Arms & Hands
**Forearms & Vambraces:**
- No rigid vambraces. Instead, natural fiber wraps (see Hands section).

**Hands — Grove-Touched Wraps (Light):**
- Construction: Undyed linen strips, approximately 3cm wide, wrapped tightly around each forearm from wrist to mid-forearm (20cm coverage).
- Wrapping pattern: Spiraling, 1.5cm gaps between wraps showing skin underneath. Wraps secured with simple tied knots at medial side of forearm.
- Color: Pale cream (#F5F3EF) with visible plant staining (faint green-brown marks, #C2A878 scattered spots suggesting Communion connection or frequent natural magic contact).
- Tactile detail: Wraps are functional (grip, support) rather than decorative. Visible wear at edges where wraps have begun to fray. Right forearm (dominant hand) shows more wear than left.
- Wrist area: Free of wrapping, allowing full wrist articulation. Visible Relic Mark location (player choice — for this reference, mark placed on inside left wrist). Mark design: organic flowing lines (pale blue-white #E8F3FF with green-gold undertone at edges, #B8D9A8), approximately 4cm across, resembles branching plant vasculature.

### Legs & Feet
**Legs — No leg armor. Bare within robe.**
- The Arcanist's Robe flows full-length, completely concealing legs.

**Feet — Grove Treads (Light):**
- Construction: Soft-soled, hand-wrapped leather shoes. Upper material: supple tan leather (#D4A574), minimal stitching (single seam down center of foot, visible stitching in pale thread).
- Sole: Crepe rubber substitute or wrapped plant fiber (appearance of layered bark, #8B8370), approximately 8mm thickness. Sole is slightly worn (visible compress marks at heel and ball of foot).
- Fastening: Simple wraparound design with single leather strap across instep, secured with toggle button (carved wood, pale aspen, same as mantle clasp).
- Detail: Sole shows evidence of regular use in natural terrain — slight mud staining visible in creases (#9C9166 accumulated debris), moss fragments caught in sole texture.
- Fit: Snug around ankle, loose through midfoot (allows significant flexion).

### Weapons
**Quarterstaff — Wellspring Timber with Arcane Integration:**
- Overall length: 1.9m (extends approximately 35cm above head when held in standing pose, slightly past outstretched arm reach).
- Shaft material: Living wood recovered from Wellspring ruins. Pale aspen base wood (#D4B8A8) with natural swirling grain pattern (darker runs of #8B7355 visible in irregular lines suggesting growth rings or Wellspring corruption patterns).
- Shaft construction: Primary shaft is smooth-worn, approximately 3cm diameter at grip area (where hand holds), tapering slightly toward both ends. Grip area shows darkening from oils (#C9A884) in two distinct hand-width sections (approximately 8cm apart, reflecting two-handed hold).
- Top node assembly (Wellspring Crystal):
  - 15cm terminal section features integrated crystalline growth (pale jade-green #B5D9C1 with luminous quality, subtle internal glow simulated via bloom).
  - Crystal nodes protrude irregularly from wood shaft (6-8 irregular geometric points, not symmetrical, suggesting natural growth rather than crafting).
  - Vines (dried plant fiber, very fine, 2-3mm diameter) wrap spirally around the crystal section in pale green (#D4E8D9), binding the crystals to wood.
  - Visible fissures in crystal sections (hairline cracks, darker interior of #7FB699 visible within breaks) suggest damage or age.
- Bottom ferrule:
  - Iron band wraps the base of the shaft approximately 4cm from bottom. Iron shows oxidation/patina (rust tones, #8B7355 to #6B5344).
  - Sharpened metal tip protrudes slightly (1cm) — functional, worn, not decorative. Metal is dull gray (#6B7280).
- Detail: The wood shows significant wear — scuffs, dings, and compressed areas where it has been dragged or struck repeatedly. This is a working tool, not a ceremonial one.

### Narrative Section
Aelira was among the Circle of Verdant Flame when the Severance shattered the Wellspring and accelerated the Aethercrown's collapse — a catastrophe she helped trigger. Now, bearing guilt that manifests as relentless precision, she wears the practical garb of a scholar and healer, her staff a living reminder of the world she damaged and the nature still reaching for her despite everything. The Wellspring crystals embedded in the staff's crown still glow faintly with a connection she's working to understand: restoration or further corruption, she cannot yet tell. Her wraps bear moss and soil from every corruption zone she's attempted to purify. The circlet was a gift from the Circle, marking her as Seer. She wears it with both reverence and the specific weight of someone who knows exactly how much her vows have cost.

## CHARACTER 2: NEWLY AWAKENED ARCHER (MEDIUM ARMOR / HOODED)

### Overview
- **Race:** Vaelari (Newly Awakened origin — emerged from chrysalis during Severance)
- **Height:** 2.0m (slightly shorter than build 1; individual variation)
- **Build:** Ectomorphic. Predatory stillness in posture. Long limbs with visible lean musculature (not bulky, but defined). Fingers tapered. Neck elongated.
- **Pose Reference:** A-stance. Arms lowered to horizontal or slightly below, elbows bent 90°, as if at rest mid-draw. Legs shoulder-width apart, slight forward weight on toes (suggests ready-to-move tension). Head turning slightly leftward (toward bow).
- **Silhouette:** Compressed vertical. Hood draws eye inward. Armor is form-fitting; no loose fabric. Silhouette reads as predatory stillness.

### Head & Face
**Facial Structure:**
- Face template: Angular. High cheekbones, narrow jaw. Sharper than Build 1 — younger appearance, still learning human expression.
- Skin tone: Pale gray-ivory with faint cool undertones (#EAEAE6). Luminescence is muted (Newly Awakened are still adjusting to severance from Wellspring — eye glow is less pronounced than Voluntary Driftless). Skin shows almost porcelain uniformity with minimal natural variation.
- Vein patterns: Visible but faint. Temples and neck show faint tracery (#D4D0E8), more subtle than Build 1.
- Eyes: Ice-blue (pupil #4A7BA7, iris color #6BA3C9, faint inner luminescence). Pupils vertically elongated (subtle, 1.2x human). Expression: alert, scanning. Pupils dilate noticeably — habit of active sensing from chrysalis memories.
- Ears: Tapered, 6.5cm length. Pale and luminescent like face. Internal cartilage detail very fine, almost translucent.
- Hair: Deep blue-black (#2B3D4A), fine texture, straight. Worn long past shoulders (waist-length when unbound) but currently contained in single tight braid secured at nape. Hair shows faint metallic-blue sheen (color variance #3D4F5A highlights). No premature silvering (unlike Voluntary Driftless) — Newly Awakened still bear marks of their emergence.
- Expression: Still, almost watchful. Eyes track movement even when head is still.

**Head Armor — Ranger's Hood (Medium):**
- Construction: Heavy wool with internal linen lining. Outer material: dark gray-brown (#4F5159), durable weight.
- Hood detail: Integrated hood covers crown and falls loosely around shoulders. Hood opening frames face with approximately 8cm border (shows face fully but shadows it significantly in rendered scene).
- Fit: Hood is worn up, covering the top of braid entirely. Braid emerges at nape, not visible from front. Lower face is fully exposed.
- Fastening: Two leather toggles (chamois-tan #D4A574) cinch the hood at front sides, allowing adjustment of opening size.
- Interior lining: Pale cream linen (#F5F3EF), visible along inside hood edge and at face opening.
- Weathering: Hood shows water staining (darker patches in #3D4F5A), fraying at raw edges, and compressed areas at nape where braid presses into fabric.

### Torso
**Chest Armor — Leather Armor (Medium):**
- Base construction: Supple, treated leather (natural tan #D4A574) fitted as form-fitting cuirass. Single-piece construction from shoulder to hip.
- Fit: Tailored to Vaelari frame — narrow shoulders, fitted through ribs, slightly flared at hip (accounts for body taper).
- Stitching: Visible topstitching in pale tan thread (single row, 5mm from seams), functional rather than decorative. Stitching shows wear (some threads slightly loose).
- Surface detail: Leather is worked but not ornate. Visible creasing across chest and sides where leather has bent repeatedly with movement. Compression marks visible at sides under arms.
- Armor points: No central reinforcement plate. Instead, leather is slightly thicker in high-impact areas (shoulders, ribs) — visible as slightly darker color variation (#C2A574) from compression.

**Shoulders — Cloak (Light):**
- Construction: Full-length cloak in heavy wool, dark gray (#3D4F5A), worn as loose drape over both shoulders.
- Attachment: Single clasp at center front — iron pin with leather backing (iron shows patina, #6B7280; leather backing is worn tan #D4A574).
- Drape detail: In A-pose with arms slightly bent, cloak drapes naturally with weight settling in folds. Approximately 6 major fold lines on each side. Cloak is longer than Build 1 mantle — extends to just above boot heel.
- Edge detail: Raw-edge hem with moderate fraying (practical, not finished). Hem shows compression staining (#2B3D4A) from ground contact.
- Hood integration: No integrated hood. Cloak is simple rectangle of fabric with shoulder drape.
- Worn appearance: Cloak shows heavy use — multiple small tears stitched with visible thread repairs (darker tan thread in contrast stitching), staining from travel (darker patches at hem and lower edges).

### Arms & Hands
**Forearms — No vambraces. Bare forearms visible within cloak.**

**Hands — Archer's Gloves (Medium):**
- Construction: Supple leather (tan #D4A574), fitted glove with reinforced palm area.
- Fit: Form-fitting through hand and wrist. Wrist cinches with small leather tie (2cm strap, buckled).
- Detail: Reinforced palm (darker leather #C2A574) with visible texturing (fine cross-hatching, shallow relief) to improve bowstring grip. Reinforcement covers entire palm and extends up two-thirds of fingers.
- Finger detail: Leather extends to fingertip but is cut short to allow full articulation and sensitivity (approximately 2cm of bare finger visible).
- Wear detail: Palm reinforcement shows significant polish (slightly shiny in spots, #B5A574 where worn smooth) from repeated bowstring contact. Visible callus-like indentations in leather where string has pressed repeatedly (three distinct groove marks on inner palm).
- Wrist area: Free of wrapping. Relic Mark location (player choice — for this reference, mark placed on inside right wrist, dominant-hand side). Mark design: organic flowing lines (pale blue-white #E8F3FF with green-gold undertone #B8D9A8), approximately 4cm across, resembles branching root structure.

### Legs & Feet
**Legs — No leg armor visible. Bare legs within leather armor and cloak.**
- Legs are completely concealed by cloak when at rest. Only visible when in active movement.

**Feet — Soft-Soled Hunting Boots (Medium):**
- Construction: Flexible leather boot with soft sole for silent movement.
- Upper material: Tan leather (#D4A574), fitted snugly around ankle and calf. Height: extends approximately 20cm above ankle (mid-calf height).
- Sole: Crepe/soft material (appears as compacted plant fiber or light rubber, #8B8370), approximately 6mm thickness. Sole is designed for silent, grip-prone movement (not heavy-footed).

- Recurve detail: Smooth curve when unstrung shows 12cm recurve at each tip (tips curve slightly toward archer when relaxed). Wood grain follows the curve, suggesting organic growth rather than heavy crafting.
- String: Plant fiber (appears as twisted pale green cord, #D4E8D9), approximately 3mm diameter. String shows stretch marks and faint discoloration (#C2A878) where archer's hand repeatedly contacts.
- Grip section: Center of bow features supple leather wrapping (tan #D4A574, approximately 8cm length). Wrapping is slightly compressed (#C2A574) where hand grips repeatedly. Two small brass rings (5mm diameter, slightly oxidized) are affixed to bow at arrow-rest position.
- Nocking point: Small carved bone nocking ring (pale cream #F5F1EC) fixed to string with sinew wrapping (visible at center).
- Limbs: Upper and lower limbs show subtle asymmetry (one limb slightly shorter/slower — typical of one-side-dominant recurve). Limb tips taper to fine points where string is nocked.
- Detail: Bow shows active use wear — small dings in wood, slight darkening on wood surface (#C9A884) from hand oils, and faint string impressions visible in wood grain where cord has rested.

**Quiver (Implied accessory, visible on torso):**
- Leather quiver (tan #D4A574) affixed to cloak/back. Worn arrow fletchings visible at quiver top (approximately 15 arrows), feathers show mix of natural colors (brown, gray, cream tones).

### Narrative Section
She remembers the moment of awakening as a moment of drowning. The chrysalis was dissolution. The Wellspring was always home, and then it shattered. Her body is barely three weeks old — muscle memory comes from instincts her mind doesn't consciously understand, and she reaches for a connection to the green that no longer answers. She learned to shoot because arrows don't require Communion, because the bow is something external and therefore survivable. Her lack of scars or aged wear seems to contradict her competence, but this is precisely what makes her dangerous: she is a predator with no childhood, no hesitation, and no memory of learning to hesitate. The Ranger's Hood is less a choice and more a necessity — the world finds her appearance unsettling, and she finds it easier to move when partially unseen.

## CHARACTER 3: HEAVY ARMOR VANGUARD (SWORD & SHIELD)

### Overview
- **Race:** Vaelari (Voluntary Driftless origin — severed from Wellspring, adopted human military culture)
- **Height:** 2.2m (tallest of the three)
- **Build:** Ectomorphic but defined. Years of military training show in postural control and subtle muscle definition. Long limbs within scaled armor. Posture reflects discipline and institutional bearing.
- **Pose Reference:** T-stance with slight forward weight shift. Arms extended horizontally, sword arm holding weight-balanced broadsword, shield arm holding kite shield. Legs shoulder-width apart. Head facing forward.
- **Silhouette:** Vertical emphasis from height, compressed by armor bulk. Shield creates additional width. Overall impression: disciplined, fortified, distinctly military.

### Head & Face
**Facial Structure:**
- Face template: Angular. High cheekbones, narrow jaw. Expression is controlled, watchful.
- Skin tone: Pale ash-gray with faint cool undertones (#E0E0DC). Luminescence is significantly reduced compared to other Vaelari (Voluntary Driftless have deliberately severed the Wellspring connection — eye and skin glow is muted). Skin tone is slightly desaturated/dulled.
- Vein patterns: Visible marble-like tracery on temples and neck (#D4D0E8), but fainter than Newly Awakened due to Driftless disconnection.
- Eyes: Silver-green, but distinctly less luminous than Build 1. Pupil color #7FB699 with notably reduced glow in iris (#A8D5BA appears as simple solid color, not luminescent). Pupils vertically elongated (subtle, 1.2x human). Expression: alert, tactical.
- Ears: Tapered, 6cm length (slightly shorter than Builds 1 and 2, possibly from minor age variation). Pale but not luminescent like other Vaelari.
- Hair: Silver-white (not ash-blonde with silver streaks — predominantly white/gray, #D4D0E0). Fine texture, straight. Worn in single tight braid secured at nape, then pinned into tight coil at back of head (military-practical style). Length: approximately shoulder-length in unbound form. Early silver (Driftless aging effect — this character is approximately 12-15 years into voluntary severance from the Wellspring).
- Expression: Disciplined, still. Eyes evaluate rather than observe.

**Head Armor — Half-Helm (Heavy):**
- Construction: Hammered iron (pale gray #6B7280 with oxidation-brown patina #8B7355 in crevices and around edges).
- Design: Standard military half-helm. Covers top of head, ears, and sides of face down to cheekbone. Opens widely at face to expose full front — shows face completely from nose down.
- Detail: Smooth-hammered surface with minimal ornamentation. Center front features narrow nasal ridge (2mm raised line running from brow to bridge of nose) for structural reinforcement.
- Fit: Sits snugly over head with internal linen padding (cream color visible at interior rim, #F5F3EF). Padding is compressed in areas where helm sits most heavily (crown, left side).
- Attachment: Leather chinstrap (tan #D4A574) fastens with brass buckle (5mm, slightly oxidized, #8B7355) under chin. Strap shows wear (leather is compressed and darkened where it sits).
- Interior construction: Visible rivets (small iron dots, #6B7280) hold interior bands of reinforcement iron. Approximately 12 rivets visible around interior circumference.
- Weathering: Helm shows active service wear — dents and dings across crown and sides (shallow deformation), scratches on front (revealing bright iron beneath patina), and water staining (darker patches #5A5F67).

### Torso
**Chest Armor — Scale Armor (Heavy):**
- Base construction: Leather cuirass (dark tan #A57344) with overlapping iron scales fixed in rows.
- Scale detail: Scales are individual iron pieces (approximately 3cm x 5cm each), hammered thin, with two small holes along top edge for affixing. Scales overlap downward like roof shingles (each scale covers the top half of the scale below it). Scales are hammered slightly curved to follow body contour.
- Scale color: Iron scales show mixed patina — some retain pale gray (#6B7280), others show rust-brown oxidation (#8B7355 to #6B5344). Variation is natural (not all scales were reforged/oiled identically).
- Stitching: Scales are affixed to leather base with dark sinew thread (visible as dark lines running horizontally between scale rows, #3D2F2A). Stitching is functional, not decorative.
- Construction layers: Under the scales, a secondary padding layer (pale linen, #F5F3EF) provides impact absorption. Linen is visible at armholes and along lower edge.
- Fit: Cuirass is fitted to Vaelari frame — narrow at shoulders, tapers slightly at waist, flares gently at hip. This reveals the contrast: human military equipment worn on an unhuman body.
- Durability detail: Several scales show dents or damage — one scale on left side is visibly cracked (hairline fracture in iron, darker #5A5F67 in the break). Scales in high-impact areas show more wear.

**Shoulders — Spaulders (Heavy):**
- Construction: Paired iron pauldrons (shoulder guards) affixed to cuirass via leather attachment straps.
- Left pauldron (non-dominant):
  - Primary structure: Curved iron plate (pale gray #6B7280) forming rounded cap over shoulder joint. Approximately 8cm tall at peak.
  - Detail: Hammered surface shows center ridge (raised line for reinforcement). Surface is mostly smooth with visible hammer marks (shallow dimpling).
  - Edge binding: Bottom edge is bound with leather (tan #D4A574) folded over and stitched to cuirass.
  - Oxidation: Minimal patina on left pauldron — appears better maintained/oiled.
- Right pauldron (dominant-arm side):
  - Same basic construction but shows more wear — visible dings and one significant dent across top (compression mark, slightly darker iron #5A5F67).
  - Oxidation is heavier on right pauldron (#8B7355 patina visible in multiple spots).
  - Binding leather is slightly worn/compressed (#C2A574).

### Arms & Hands
**Forearms — No separate vambraces. Bare forearms visible at wrist.**

**Hands — Iron Gauntlets (Heavy):**
- Construction: Articulated plate gauntlets (gauntlet definition: hand and wrist protection with iron plates over knuckles and back of hand, leather palm).
- Hand plate detail:

### Narrative Section
He was Driftless by choice, not accident — severed the Wellspring connection deliberately twelve years ago because he wanted something the green could not give: structure, purpose, order, and the certainty that comes from serving something beyond instinct. He joined the Vanguard and rose to Captain because he was exceptionally good at being human, at least in the ways that mattered to military hierarchy. The Severance shattered that order entirely. Now he holds the line because letting go means admitting the choice he made was catastrophic — that by trying to build a different life, he abandoned the one people who looked like him needed. His Relic Mark is clean and deliberate (scar-like, not organic) because he enforced the severance with ritual, with intention. Every dent and rust-patch on his armor is a weight he chose to carry, and his discipline is the only thing preventing him from setting it down.

## Reference Implementation Notes

### For 3D Asset Extraction
1. **Modeling order suggestion:** Head → Torso → Limbs → Armor → Weapons
2. **T-Pose/A-Pose rigging:** All three builds assume neutral standing pose for reference. Joint articulation in gameplay will deform these silhouettes (armor will crease, layered pieces will shift, weapons will reposition).
3. **Subsurface scattering:** All Vaelari skin requires SSS shader (faint internal glow, especially ears and thin areas around eyes). Build 1 and Build 2 have stronger SSS than Build 3 (Driftless severance reduces luminescence).
4. **Metal weathering:** Use mixed diffuse and normal maps for iron oxidation (patina is not flat — oxidized areas have slightly raised surface texture).
5. **Leather wear:** Compression mapping is critical for leather hand contact areas (palms, grips, where straps sit). Use detail maps to create worn-smooth vs. raw texture variation.
6. **Armor articulation:** Scale, chainmail, plate armor will deform differently during animation. Plan rigging to accommodate plate-over-leather weight distribution and scale overlapping behavior.
